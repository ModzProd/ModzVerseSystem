News & Updates

The news feed is the intranet's chronological log of significant world events, database changes, and project milestones. Items are tagged by category (SYSTEM, BETA, LORE, DATA) and dated by canon date or build date. This is the place to look when you need to know what has changed since the last time you opened the intranet.

January 2025 — Canon Launch

SYSTEM2025-01-01 · canon launch
The ModzVerse canon year officially begins. World state is initialized to January 1, 2025. The gameworld table records: startDate='2025-01-01', currentDate='2025-01-01', databaseVersion=121, workerGeneration=1, sandboxMode=0, advancedStats=0, advancedPopularityMode=0, ownerGoalsEnabled=0. All gameplay toggles are at their default (off) state, ready for the player to enable as desired.
LORE2025-01-01 · tagline
The world's tagline is officially "A World at War!". This is not marketing copy — it is the structural thesis of the world. Every promotion in the ModzVerse is positioned in active conflict with at least one other entity. The four documented conflicts are: the Lucha Civil War (Lucha Bellas vs. Lucha MEX), the Japanese Division Wars (RAIBARAZU internal brand war + Osaka Joshi vs. Valkyrie Project cold war), the Chicago Anomaly (Chicago Show's geographic isolation), and the Continental Vacuum (Europe, Africa, South America, Oceania have no home promotions). See World Lore for the full conflict matrix.
DATA2025-01-01 · promotions
All six promotions are confirmed Active in the database. Founder distribution: Chicago Show (1950-01-01), RAIBARAZU (1950-06-06), Lucha Bellas (1980-01-01), Lucha MEX (1980-01-01), Valkyrie Project (1980-01-01), Osaka Joshi (2003-01-01). Three promotions share the same founding date (1980-01-01) — Lucha Bellas, Lucha MEX, and Valkyrie Project — a coincidence that the world's kayfabe history treats as no coincidence at all.
DATA2025-01-01 · championship
The AEGIS LEAGUE championship is confirmed vacant as of canon launch. The title's currentChampion, currentChampion2, and currentChampion3 fields are empty strings. The title's defendable=0 flag means it does not have to be defended on any particular schedule — it is defended only at the three Valkyrie Project signature events (Valhalla, Twilight of The GODS, AEGIS LEAGUE Finals). The first Valkyrie Project player will need to crown the first champion, most likely via a tournament at Valhalla (January Week 1, the first event of the canon year).
DATA2025-01-01 · calendar bottleneck
The January Week 5 bottleneck is confirmed. Three supershows are scheduled on the same weekend: Battle of Rivals (RAIBARAZU, 120 min), Luchastica (Lucha Bellas, 90 min), and The AEGIS LEAGUE Finals (Valkyrie Project, 30 min). The simulation will track viewership and attendance for all three events, and the loser of that three-way contest will start the canon year with a narrative deficit. Players booking any of these three promotions should plan around the bottleneck.
BETApending · roster
The worker roster is empty across all 6 promotions. The world database has 0 entries in the workers, contracts, venues, networks, networkDeals, matchgimmicks, matchstories, angleTemplates, templateBeats, tagteams, stables, and storylines tables. The structural foundation is complete; the human layer is not. Estimated roster needed for v1.0: 112-180 workers. See BETA Status for the full gap analysis.
BETApending · Lucha MEX events
Lucha MEX and Osaka Joshi — both with non-zero regional popularity — have no signature events scheduled. Lucha MEX has North American popularity 40 (the second-highest regional score in the world) but no annual supershow. Osaka Joshi has the only non-zero prestige score in the world (38) but no annual supershow. Both promotions need at least one signature event added before v1.0 launch.
DATA2025-01-01 · RAIBARAZU tours
Three RAIBARAZU touring cycles are pre-scheduled in the eventDates table: Road to Autumn Blood Moon (Sep 2-27, 12 stops, all in Japan), Road to Fall of Honor (Oct 1-31, 13 stops, all in Japan), and Road to Frozen Combat (Feb 3-28, 12 stops, all in Japan). The Road to Battle of Rivals tour has no pre-scheduled dates — either dynamically scheduled or a BETA-era gap. Two tours are autobooked, two require manual booking.
LORE2025-01-01 · brand mythology
The RAIBARAZU brand split is officially documented: Doragon (ドラゴン, "Dragon") is the A-brand, the flagship roster with the top stars; Fenikkusu (フェニックス, "Phoenix") is the B-brand, the developmental and undercard roster. The choice of the dragon and the phoenix is meaningful: in East Asian mythology, the dragon (ryū) symbolizes strength and imperial authority, while the phoenix symbolizes rebirth. Workers on Fenikkusu are workers being reborn — fighting for the call-up to Doragon that represents their professional resurrection.

This suggests a plugin (likely starting with "pr_" — PromoRevenue, ProRefs, or similar) has been run against the database at least once. The modder should be aware of this when sharing the database publicly, as the token may need to be cleared for a clean v1.0 release.
DATA · RELEASE2025-01-01 · venue packs
The ModzVerse project has released 10 venue database packs alongside the canon launch. Each pack is a separate SQLite database file containing a different slice of the world's venue dataset — 4 regional packs (NorthAmerica, Africa, Asia, Europe), 3 capacity-tier packs (Arena 20,000+ seats, Large 10,000-19,950 seats, Medium 5,000-9,975 seats), and 3 style-specific packs (Hardcore, Kings Road, Lucha Libre). Together they total 16,859 venue records spanning all six continents. The packs are at schema version 103 (older than the main world's v121) and should be loaded with the migration handler. See Venue Database Packs.
LORE2025-01-01 · Australia insight
Analysis of the venue packs reveals a surprising structural finding: Australia is the world's leading market for two styles that have no home promotion in the launch roster. Australia hosts 135 of the 332 Hardcore-preferred venues (41% of the global Hardcore venue footprint) and 169 of the 257 Kings Road-preferred venues (66%). Australia also leads the Large capacity pack (438 venues) and the Medium capacity pack (433 venues). Despite having zero home promotions, Australia has the deepest venue infrastructure per capita in the ModzVerse — a structural opportunity for any player considering a Hardcore or Kings Road promotion. See Cross-Pack Insights.
DATA2025-01-01 · venue schema
All 10 venue packs share a 14-column schema: venueID, description, region, country, venueName, capacity, wrestlingPopularity, preferredStyle, promotionExclusivity, openDate, closeDate, type, venueImage, continent. Capacity ranges from 125 (smallest venue, in North America) to 44,750 (Pennsylvania Elite Dome, USA). Wrestling popularity ranges from 8 (lowest, in Asia) to 95 (highest, in North America). Opening dates span 1900 to 2024 — over 124 years of wrestling venue history. ~5% of venues are historically closed; ~5% have promotion exclusivity agreements. See Venue Schema Reference.
DATA · EXPANSION2025-01-01 · 8 new venue packs
The ModzVerse project has expanded the venue pack collection from 10 to 18 packs, bringing the total venue count from 16,859 to 36,000 venues. The 8 new packs are: South America (2,000 venues, Brazil leads with 895), Oceania (2,000, Australia leads with 1,150), Small capacity (2,310 venues, 2,000-4,975 seats), Tiny capacity (966 venues, under 2,000 seats), Pure Wrestling style (396 venues), Sports Entertainment style (3,568 — the largest style pack), Strong Style (307), and Traditional style (6,594 — the largest pack of all 18). The expansion completes both the regional pack coverage (now all 6 continents) and the style pack coverage (now all 7 canonical styles). See Venue Database Packs.
LORE2025-01-01 · Australia × 4 styles
With the addition of the Pure Wrestling and Strong Style packs, the "Australia Surprise" finding has expanded: Australia is now the leading market for four of the seven wrestling styles — Hardcore (135 venues, 41% of pack), Kings Road (169, 66%), Pure Wrestling (149, 38%), and Strong Style (129, 42%). Australia also leads the Large capacity pack (438 venues), Medium pack (433), and the new Oceania regional pack (1,150 venues — 57.5% of the pack). Despite having zero home promotions in the launch roster, Australia is structurally the most underserved wrestling market in the ModzVerse — and the most strategically valuable target for any new promotion. See Cross-Pack Insights.
DATA2025-01-01 · Traditional dominance
The new Traditional style pack is the largest of all 18 venue packs with 6,594 venues — nearly double the size of the Sports Entertainment pack (3,568). The pack is concentrated in developing-world markets: India (598 venues), China (575), Papua New Guinea (456), Russia (317), and Nigeria (295). The pack's average wrestling popularity of 38 is the lowest of any style pack, reflecting the prevalence of Traditional-preference venues in less wrestling-saturated markets. This data clarifies a structural ModzVerse dynamic: the Traditional style is the global default — the style most venues in the world prefer — but it is also the style with the lowest average fan passion. Lucha MEX, the only promotion practicing Traditional, has structural access to the largest venue pool in the world but faces the lowest average fan engagement.

Each DLC database is a complete, self-contained SQLite file with all 78 tables from the main ModzVerse.db — only the filtered table differs. See Match Gimmicks DLC and TV Networks DLC.

The repository now contains 4 Excel reference spreadsheets documenting the ModzVerse world in human-readable form: ModzVerse_Promotions.xlsx (5,000 promotions × 3 columns), ModzVerse_All_Titles.xlsx (5,805 titles × 18 columns with prestige, gender, weight, push, defendable, active), ModzVerse_All_Promotions_AI_Prompts.xlsx (4,530 promotions × 14 columns including AI logo generation prompts — original + improved versions for each), and ModzVerse_All_Venues_AI_Prompts.xlsx (12,000 venues × 13 columns including AI image generation prompts — original + improved). Total: 33,060 AI prompts ready to paste into any image generator. See Excel Resources.
LORE2025-01-01 · Brazil Network Surprise
Analysis of the TV Networks DLC reveals a second "Brazil surprise" to complement the venue pack finding: Brazil has 5 size-3 (Large) national TV networks — Canal Nacional, Sol Channel, Rio Channel, Luta TV Brasil, and Rede Luta Livre. This is the most size-3 networks of any single country in the ModzVerse. Combined with Brazil's 895 venues (the most of any country in the South America regional pack) and 215 million population, Brazil is the single most strategically valuable untapped wrestling market in the world — broadcast infrastructure is ready and waiting, no other untapped market comes close. See TV Networks DLC.
