Match Stories (8 templates)

Each match story template includes phase-by-phase text beats (Opening → Middle → Climax → Finish) that the simulation uses to generate play-by-play commentary during matches.

#Match TypeDescription
1Standard Singles MatchA standard one-on-one singles match with pinfall or submission finish. 6 text beats covering opening tie-ups, mid-match hold exchanges, climax near-falls, and finisher pin.
2Hardcore BrawlA no-disqualification match with weapons and brawling. 6 beats including steel chair introduction, outside brawling, table setup, blood, and table-break finish.
3Tag Team MatchA standard tag team match with hot tags and double-team moves. 5 beats covering face isolation, heel cheating, face-in-peril crawl, HOT TAG sequence, and double-team finish.
4Lucha Libre MatchA high-flying lucha libre match with dives and rapid exchanges. 5 beats with rapid arm drags, tope suicida, hurricanrana near-fall, and springboard splash finish.
5Monster SquashA dominant performance by a monster heel against enhancement talent. 4 beats — stare down, devastating clothesline, finisher, sub-2-minute pin.
6Championship MatchA high-stakes championship match with near falls and dramatic kickouts. 5 beats including belt presentation, finisher reversal, champion's finisher kickout, and retaining pin.
7Mask vs Mask (Luchastica)The most dramatic match in lucha libre — the loser loses their mask. 5 beats with identity tension, brutal exchanges, mask violation attempt, and unmasking finish.
8Iron Man MatchA 30-minute iron man match with multiple falls. 5 beats with opening announcement, first fall, comeback, tied climax, and last-second winner.

★ The Storyline Architecture
With 21 storylines across 6 promotions, the ModzVerse provides a booking player with months of narrative material from day one. Every promotion has a headline feud (the ace vs the top heel), a stable war (the heel faction vs the face faction), and a unique narrative arc — from RAIBARAZU's call-up quest (a B-brand worker fighting for promotion to the A-brand) to Lucha MEX's mask collection (a heel systematically unmasking technicos) to Valkyrie Project's AEGIS LEAGUE tournament (friendship tested by competition). Combined with 8 match story templates and 15 angle templates, a booking player has everything needed to run a full year of shows without generating any content from scratch.
