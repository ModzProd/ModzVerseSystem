The world's tagline — "A World at War!" — is the structural premise, not marketing copy. Four conflicts define the opening state: Lucha Civil War, Japanese Division Wars, Chicago Anomaly, Continental Vacuum. See World Lore.

Database is at schema version 121, newer than the 70+ era in baseline PWS codebase. Advanced regional popularity mode is disabled in the world header (set to advancedPopularityMode=0); can be toggled on. See BETA Status for known issues.
