Geography of the ModzVerse

The ModzVerse contains the most granular geographic dataset of any wrestling simulation world: 190 countries and 3,218 regions, each with its own population, economy, dictatorship status, wrestling popularity score, and preferred wrestling style. This is not just a backdrop — it is an active simulation layer that determines how much a promotion earns when it books a show in a given region, how much a worker's regional popularity grows when they win over a local crowd, and which styles of wrestling will resonate with which fanbases.

Every country on Earth is represented, from the largest (China, population 1.4 billion) to the smallest (Vatican City, population 800). Every region within those countries — every state in the United States, every prefecture in Japan, every province in Canada — has its own data row. The result is a world where a booking decision in Ontario, Canada (population 14.9 million, Strong Style preference, wrestling popularity 90) is mechanically distinct from a booking decision in Quebec (population 8.6 million, Lucha Libre preference, wrestling popularity 75), even though both are in the same country.

Continent-Level Breakdown

The simulation tracks popularity at the continent level across six regions: North America, South America, Oceania, Asia, Europe, and Africa. The table below summarizes each continent's wrestling landscape, including the home promotions (if any) and the key countries within each continent.

NA
North America
3 home promotions · key countries: USA, Mexico, Canada, Cuba, Dominican Republic

North America is the most promotion-dense continent in the ModzVerse, hosting three of the world's six promotions: Chicago Show (USA), Lucha Bellas (Mexico), and Lucha MEX (Mexico). The continent has the deepest wrestling culture in the world — Canada in particular has the highest regional popularity scores (Ontario: 90, Alberta: 85, British Columbia: 78), reflecting the country's deep wrestling tradition. Mexico is the home of lucha libre, with regional preferences split between Lucha Libre, Traditional, and Hardcore styles depending on the region. The United States is the home of Sports Entertainment, with Chicago Show as the lone promotion.

SA
South America
0 home promotions · key countries: Brazil, Argentina, Colombia, Venezuela, Peru

South America has no home promotions in the ModzVerse — the entire continent is part of the "continental vacuum" that long-term expansionist promotions can claim. Despite the lack of native promotions, South America has meaningful wrestling popularity in several countries: Brazil (population 215 million, wrestling popularity 70, Sports Entertainment preference), Argentina (population 45.8 million, wrestling popularity 50, Sports Entertainment preference), and Colombia (population 52 million, wrestling popularity 50, Sports Entertainment preference). Brazil alone represents one of the largest untapped wrestling markets in the world — 215 million people with a 70 wrestling popularity score and no home promotion to serve them.

No home promotions — continental vacuum
OC
Oceania
0 home promotions · key countries: Australia, New Zealand, Fiji, Papua New Guinea

Oceania is the smallest continent in the ModzVerse by both population and wrestling relevance. Australia is the only meaningful market (population 26 million), but even Australia has a modest wrestling popularity score that limits the continent's commercial potential. No home promotions exist in Oceania; the continent is part of the continental vacuum, but its small population and modest wrestling culture make it a low-priority expansion target compared to South America or Europe.

No home promotions — continental vacuum
AS
Asia
3 home promotions · key countries: Japan, India, China, South Korea, Indonesia

Asia is the most wrestling-saturated continent in the ModzVerse, hosting three of the world's six promotions — all Japanese. RAIBARAZU (Tokyo), Osaka Joshi (Osaka), and Valkyrie Project (Saga) all compete for the same Asian fanbase, with RAIBARAZU holding the largest share (Asian popularity 60, the highest regional popularity score in the world). Japan itself is the world's most concentrated wrestling market — every region in Japan has a wrestling popularity score, and the regional preferences span all seven of the canonical wrestling styles, making Japan a microcosm of the entire global wrestling scene.

Beyond Japan, Asia contains the world's largest untapped markets: India (population 1.4 billion), China (population 1.4 billion), and Indonesia (population 275 million). None of these countries have a home promotion, and their wrestling popularity scores are modest, but their sheer population sizes make them long-term strategic prizes for any promotion willing to invest in building a fanbase from scratch.

EU
Europe
0 home promotions · key countries: UK, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Russia

Europe is the largest continent in the ModzVerse by number of countries (over 50) and the second-largest by population (over 750 million). Despite this, no home promotions exist in Europe — the entire continent is part of the continental vacuum. The UK (population 67 million), Germany (population 84 million), France (population 65 million), and Italy (population 59 million) are all meaningful markets with non-trivial wrestling popularity scores, but none have a native promotion. Europe represents the single largest untapped wrestling market in the ModzVerse by population, and the first promotion to establish a European touring circuit could capture a fanbase larger than any single promotion's current regional base.

No home promotions — continental vacuum
AF
Africa
0 home promotions · key countries: Nigeria, Ethiopia, Egypt, South Africa, DR Congo

Africa is the second-largest continent by population (over 1.3 billion) and contains several countries with surprisingly high wrestling popularity scores. Nigeria (population 218 million), Egypt (population 109 million), and South Africa (population 60 million) are the largest markets, but wrestling popularity scores across Africa are generally lower than in other continents, reflecting the historical lack of professional wrestling infrastructure. Africa is a long-term strategic prize — the population is there, but the wrestling culture would need to be built from scratch. No home promotions exist in Africa.

No home promotions — continental vacuum

Top Wrestling Markets: Country Spotlight

Below are spotlights on the five most strategically important countries in the ModzVerse, based on a combination of population, wrestling popularity, and the presence (or absence) of home promotions. These are the countries a booking player should think about most carefully when planning international expansion.

Japan — The Wrestling Capital

Population125,800,000
Economy90 / 100
Wrestling Popularity85 (avg)
Preferred StyleKings Road
Home Promotions3 (RAIBARAZU, Osaka Joshi, Valkyrie Project)
Strategic Value★★★★★

Japan is the wrestling capital of the ModzVerse. Three of the world's six promotions are based here, the country has the highest regional popularity scores of any nation (Ontario, Canada scores 90 — but Ontario is a single region; Japan has multiple regions scoring 80+), and the country's regional preferences span the full range of wrestling styles, making it a microcosm of the global wrestling landscape. A booking player who can establish dominance in Japan — or who can successfully expand a non-Japanese promotion into the Japanese market — will have access to the most wrestling-saturated fanbase on Earth.

United States — The Rich Outpost

Population331,000,000
Economy95 / 100
Wrestling Popularity~65 (varies by region)
Preferred StyleSports Entertainment
Home Promotions1 (Chicago Show)
Strategic Value★★★★☆

The United States is the world's largest economy and the third-most populous country, but it hosts only one home promotion (Chicago Show). The country's regional preferences skew heavily toward Sports Entertainment — the style that Chicago Show practices — which means Chicago Show has a structural advantage in its home market. The country's large population and high economy make it a lucrative market for any promotion that can break through, but the Sports Entertainment preference limits the appeal of Japanese and Mexican promotions that might try to expand into the US.

Mexico — The Lucha Stronghold

Population128,900,000
Economy65 / 100
Wrestling Popularity80 (national avg)
Preferred StyleLucha Libre
Home Promotions2 (Lucha Bellas, Lucha MEX)
Strategic Value★★★★☆

Mexico is the home of lucha libre and hosts two of the world's six promotions. The country's regional preferences are split between Lucha Libre (the dominant style in the south and central regions) and Traditional (the style favored in the northern regions, reflecting the influence of border-state wrestling with the US). Mexico's wrestling popularity is among the highest in the world, making it one of the most lucrative markets per capita. The two home promotions — Lucha Bellas and Lucha MEX — are locked in a civil war for control of this fanbase, and the winner will have a structural advantage that may take decades to dislodge.

Brazil — The Untapped Giant

Population215,350,000
Economy70 / 100
Wrestling Popularity70
Preferred StyleSports Entertainment
Home Promotions0
Strategic Value★★★★★

Brazil is the single largest untapped wrestling market in the ModzVerse. With a population of 215 million, an economy score of 70, a wrestling popularity of 70, and a Sports Entertainment style preference, Brazil is essentially a "second United States" with no incumbent promotion. The first promotion to establish a Brazilian touring circuit — most likely Chicago Show, given the Sports Entertainment style match — could capture a fanbase that no other promotion can contest. Brazil is the most strategically valuable country in the continental vacuum.

Canada — The Highest-Pop Market

Population39,570,000
Economy85 / 100
Wrestling Popularity70 (national)
Preferred StyleSports Entertainment
Home Promotions0
Strategic Value★★★★☆

Canada is, on paper, the most wrestling-saturated country in the ModzVerse. The national wrestling popularity score is 70, but the regional scores are much higher — Ontario (population 14.9 million) has a wrestling popularity of 90, the highest single-region score in the world. Alberta (population 4.5 million) scores 85, British Columbia (population 5.3 million) scores 78. Despite this, no home promotion exists in Canada — the entire country is part of the continental vacuum. Chicago Show, with its Sports Entertainment style preference match and its geographic proximity, is the obvious candidate to capture the Canadian market. The first promotion to tour Ontario regularly will have access to the most wrestling-passionate fanbase on Earth.

Style Preferences by Region

The ModzVerse tracks style preferences at both the country and region level. The seven canonical wrestling styles — Sports Entertainment, Lucha Libre, Traditional, Pure Wrestling, Kings Road, Hardcore, and Strong Style — are distributed across the world's regions in patterns that reflect real-world wrestling history. The Style Paradox
Two of the seven canonical styles — Strong Style and Hardcore — have regional strongholds but no home promotion in the ModzVerse. This creates a structural opportunity: a player who creates a Strong Style promotion based in Alberta (pop 85) or a Hardcore promotion based in Chihuahua (pop 80) would have access to a regional fanbase that no existing promotion can credibly serve. The style preferences exist; the promotions do not. This is one of the most underexploited strategic angles in the entire ModzVerse.
