Venue Database Packs

★ UPDATED · v0.9.3 — 18 Venue Packs Now Available The ModzVerse project has released 18 venue database packs, each containing a different slice of the world's venue dataset. Together they total 36,000 venue records across all six continents, all seven wrestling styles, and every inhabited region on Earth. The packs are organized along three axes — geography (6 regional packs), capacity tier (5 packs), and preferred style (7 packs) — and are designed to be loaded individually or combined. A player booking a North American promotion can load just the NorthAmerica pack, while a player running a global touring circuit can combine all six regional packs.

The 18 Venue Packs at a Glance

The packs are organized along three axes: geographic region (6 packs covering all inhabited continents), capacity tier (5 packs spanning tiny to arena-scale venues), and preferred style (7 packs, one for each canonical wrestling style). Each pack is a separate SQLite database file based on the same world template, with only the venues table differing. The schema is identical across all packs (14 columns: venueID, description, region, country, venueName, capacity, wrestlingPopularity, preferredStyle, promotionExclusivity, openDate, closeDate, type, venueImage, continent).

Regional Packs (6 files · 12,000 venues)

The six regional packs are the foundation of the venue dataset. Each one contains exactly 2,000 venues located in a specific continent, with no overlap between packs. Together they cover every inhabited continent on Earth. These packs are designed for promotions running shows in a specific geographic area — a Chicago Show player loads NorthAmerica, a RAIBARAZU player loads Asia, a hypothetical Australian Hardcore promotion loads Oceania, and so on.

The North America pack covers the United States (1,018 venues), Mexico (540), Canada (146), Guatemala (68), Haiti (42), and the rest of the Caribbean and Central America. It is the highest-popularity regional pack (average 73 out of 100), reflecting North America's deep wrestling culture — particularly the United States and Canada, where regional wrestling popularity scores are the highest in the world (Ontario: 90, Alberta: 85, Pennsylvania: 92). The pack's dominant preferred style is Sports Entertainment (1,156 venues), reflecting the regional preference of the United States, with significant Lucha Libre (250) and Traditional (294) representation from Mexico.

Top venues: Pennsylvania Elite Dome (cap 44,750, pop 92, Sports Entertainment), Silver Maryland Megaplex (cap 44,700, pop 85), Auditorium of United States (cap 44,500, pop 82, Massachusetts). The pack includes 107 venues with promotion exclusivity agreements and 101 historically closed venues. The opening dates span from 1900 to December 2024, covering 124 years of North American wrestling venue history.

Capacity Tier Packs (5 files · 12,000 venues)

The five capacity-tier packs are organized by seating capacity rather than geography. Together they span every venue size from 75 seats (a tiny community hall) to 44,750 seats (the Pennsylvania Elite Dome). They are designed for players who want to book shows of a specific size — an intimate indie show (Tiny pack), a small regional event (Small pack), a standard weekly TV taping (Medium pack), a standard PPV (Large pack), or a stadium-scale supershow (Arena pack). Because these packs span all continents, they overlap heavily with the regional packs.

Style-Specific Packs (7 files · 11,800 venues)

The seven style-specific packs filter venues by their preferredStyle field rather than by geography or capacity. Together they cover all seven canonical wrestling styles in the ModzVerse. These packs are designed for players running promotions that practice a specific style — a Hardcore promotion needs Hardcore-friendly venues, a Lucha Libre promotion needs Lucha Libre-friendly venues. The style packs are particularly valuable for the two styles that have no home promotion in the ModzVerse's launch roster: Hardcore and Strong Style.

Cross-Pack Insights

★ The Australia Surprise — Now Confirmed Across 4 Styles
The single most striking finding from the expanded venue pack dataset is that Australia is the world's leading market for four of the seven wrestling styles: Hardcore (135 of 332 venues, 41%), Kings Road (169 of 257, 66%), Pure Wrestling (149 of 396, 38%), and Strong Style (129 of 307, 42%). Australia also has the highest venue count in the Large capacity pack (438 venues), the Medium pack (433), and the Oceania regional pack (1,150 of 2,000 venues, 57.5%). Despite having zero home promotions in the launch roster, Australia has the deepest venue infrastructure per capita of any country in the ModzVerse. A player looking to start a Hardcore, Kings Road, Pure Wrestling, or Strong Style promotion — particularly one targeting the underserved Australian market — has a structural advantage that no existing promotion can contest.
Capacity Tier Distribution — Full Coverage
The five capacity packs now provide full coverage of every venue size in the ModzVerse: 966 tiny venues (under 2,000 seats) for indie shows and training schools, 2,310 small venues (2,000-4,975 seats) for developmental territories, 2,969 medium venues (5,000-9,975 seats) for weekly TV tapings and house shows, 3,660 large venues (10,000-19,950 seats) for PPVs and major events, and 2,095 arena-scale venues (20,000+ seats) for supershows and stadium tours. The Large pack has the most venues, suggesting the world's venue infrastructure is optimized for the 10,000-20,000 seat range — the sweet spot for profitable wrestling events. The capacity distribution follows a roughly bell-shaped curve, peaking at the Large tier.
Style Distribution — Traditional Dominates Globally
Aggregating the style preferences across all 18 packs (with overlaps): Traditional is the most common style preference globally with 6,594 venues (the entire Traditional pack), followed by Sports Entertainment with 3,568 venues, Lucha Libre with 546, Pure Wrestling with 396, Hardcore with 332, Strong Style with 307, and Kings Road with 257. The two styles with no home promotion in the ModzVerse launch — Hardcore and Strong Style — have meaningful venue footprints (332 and 307 venues respectively), suggesting latent demand for promotions practicing these styles. Combined with the Pure Wrestling pack (396 venues) and Kings Road pack (257 venues), these four "non-mainstream" styles total 1,292 venues — a substantial underserved market.
Regional Distribution — Full Six-Continent Coverage
With the addition of the South America and Oceania regional packs, the venue dataset now provides complete coverage of every inhabited continent. Each regional pack contains exactly 2,000 venues, for a total of 12,000 regional-pack venues. The breakdown: North America (2,000, avg pop 73 — highest), South America (2,000, avg pop 44), Africa (2,000, avg pop 35 — lowest), Asia (2,000, avg pop 42), Europe (2,000, avg pop 48), and Oceania (2,000, avg pop 42). North America's wrestling popularity dominance is clear: the average NA venue has a popularity score 2.1x higher than the average African venue, reflecting the deep cultural entrenchment of professional wrestling in North America versus the developing wrestling markets of Africa.

Every venue across all 18 packs uses the same 14-column schema. The table below documents each column.

descriptionTEXT (200)Short human-readable description, e.g., "Grand ballroom with ornate decor located in Mexico".

How to Use the Venue Packs

The venue packs are designed to be loaded into a ModzVerse save file individually or in combination. The recommended approach depends on the player's booking strategy:

  • Single-region promotion: Load only the regional pack matching the promotion's home continent. A Chicago Show player loads NorthAmerica; a RAIBARAZU player loads Asia; a hypothetical Australian Hardcore promotion loads Oceania. This keeps the venue list manageable (2,000 venues) and ensures every show is bookable locally.
  • Touring promotion: Load the regional packs for every continent the promotion tours. A RAIBARAZU player running international tours would load Asia (home), NorthAmerica (for US tours), Europe (for European tours), and Oceania (for Australian tours). This expands the venue list to 8,000+ but enables global touring.
  • Style-specific promotion: Load the style pack matching the promotion's style. A Hardcore promotion loads the Hardcore pack (332 venues); a Lucha Libre promotion loads the Lucha Libre pack (546); a Pure Wrestling promotion loads the Pure Wrestling pack (396). These venues will draw better for shows of that style than randomly-selected venues.
  • Capacity-matched promotion: Load the capacity pack matching the promotion's typical show size. A promotion running weekly TV shows loads the Medium pack (2,969 venues, 5,000-9,975 seats); a promotion running monthly supershows loads the Arena pack (2,095 venues, 20,000+ seats); an indie promotion running small shows loads the Small or Tiny pack (2,310 or 966 venues respectively).
  • Comprehensive load: Load all 18 packs. This gives access to every venue in the dataset (36,000 records, with overlaps deduplicated by venueID) but produces a very long venue picker list. Recommended only for players who want maximum booking flexibility.
⚠ Schema Version Note
All 18 venue pack databases are at schema version 103, while the original ModzVerse(BETA).db is at schema version 121. This means the venue packs are based on an older version of the world template. When loading a venue pack into a v121 save file, the simulation will run a migration to bring the venue pack's schema up to v121 — this is automatic in PWS but may take a few seconds. Do not be alarmed if you see migration messages in the console when loading a venue pack for the first time.
⚠ Other Tables in Venue Pack DBs
Each venue pack database contains more than just the venues table — they also include pre-populated promotions (5,000 entries), gimmicks (15,303 entries), titles (5,505 entries), and networks (101 entries) tables from the baseline template. These are not the canonical ModzVerse promotions/gimmicks/titles — they are template data that should be ignored or overwritten when merging the venue pack into a ModzVerse save file. Only the venues table should be imported; the other tables in the venue pack DBs are scaffolding.
