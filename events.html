The Events Calendar

The ModzVerse calendar is structured around 13 signature events distributed across the year, hosted by four of the six promotions (Chicago Show, Lucha Bellas, RAIBARAZU, and Valkyrie Project — Lucha MEX and Osaka Joshi do not currently host signature events). These events are the focal points of the world's booking year — the moments when storylines pay off, championships are contested, and cross-promotional rivalries are activated. The calendar is anchored by three clusters of "supershow weekends" where multiple promotions run competing events on the same date.

Events in the ModzVerse fall into three structural categories:

  • Annual events — recurring supershows scheduled for a specific week of a specific month each year. These are the promotion's signature moments, the equivalent of WrestleMania or Wrestle Kingdom.
  • Tour events — recurring touring circuits that run for several weeks, with multiple stops along the way. Tours are typically used to build toward an annual supershow; they are the "Road to…" structure that RAIBARAZU uses to feed its three big shows.
  • Weekly events — recurring shows that air every week on a specific day. Chicago Show is the only promotion running a weekly event; it is the ModzVerse equivalent of a weekly TV show.

All 13 events are configured with "Huge" importance — the highest tier in the simulation's importance scale. This means every signature event in the ModzVerse is, by design, a major moment for the promotion hosting it. There are no "B-shows" in the canonical calendar; the simulation will treat every one of these events as a potential turning point in the world's narrative.

The January Week 5 Bottleneck
Three of the world's biggest supershows — Battle of Rivals (RAIBARAZU), Luchastica (Lucha Bellas), and The AEGIS LEAGUE Finals (Valkyrie Project) — are all scheduled for January Week 5. This is the most crowded date on the ModzVerse calendar and the single biggest booking conflict of the canon year. A worker who is featured on one of these shows cannot appear on another; a fan who wants to attend cannot be in three places at once. The simulation will track viewership and attendance for all three events, and the loser of that three-way contest will start the canon year with a narrative deficit.

The RAIBARAZU Tour System Explained

RAIBARAZU is the only promotion in the ModzVerse that uses a touring-circuit model. The promotion runs three annual supershows — Battle of Rivals, Autumn Blood Moon, and Fall of Honor — and each is fed by a dedicated touring cycle in the weeks leading up to the show. The tour serves as the qualifying circuit: workers compete on tour stops to earn their spot on the supershow, and the results of the tour directly determine the card of the supershow.

The tour system is configured in the database with the recurrenceType='Tour' setting, distinct from the 'Annual' and 'Weekly' settings used by other events. Each tour is configured with repeatAnnually=1, meaning the same tour structure recurs year after year, and individual tour stops are pre-scheduled in the eventDates table with specific dates, preferred continents, preferred countries, and preferred regions.

The pre-scheduled tour dates in the ModzVerse database (38 entries in the eventDates table) show two complete tour cycles:

  • Road to Autumn Blood Moon — September 2 to September 27 (12 stops, every 2–3 days, all in Asia / Japan / countryID 137)
  • Road to Fall of Honor — October 1 to October 31, 2025 (13 stops, every 2–3 days, all in Asia / Japan / countryID 137)
  • Road to Frozen Combat — February 3 to February 28, 2025 (12 stops, every 2–3 days, all in Asia / Japan / countryID 137)

Notably, all tour stops are currently preferred for the same location — Asia / Japan. This is a BETA-era limitation; a fully-realized RAIBARAZU tour would likely include stops in other Asian countries (South Korea, Taiwan, the Philippines) and potentially international stops in North America and Europe as the promotion expands its reach.

The autobook configuration varies across the three tours. Road to Autumn Blood Moon and Road to Battle of Rivals have autobook=1, meaning the simulation will book them automatically. Road to Fall of Honor and Road to Frozen Combat have autobook=0, meaning the player must book them manually. This asymmetry is unusual and may be a BETA-era oversight; it suggests the modder intends for some tours to be self-running while others require player attention.

Event Profiles: By Promotion

Chicago Show — Weekly

The Chicago Show weekly event is the only weekly event in the ModzVerse. It airs every Sunday evening, runs for 60 minutes, and is configured with "Huge" importance — meaning every weekly show is treated by the simulation as a major event. This is unusual: in most wrestling simulations, weekly events are treated as lower-importance TV shows that build toward monthly or annual supershows. In the ModzVerse, Chicago Show's weekly event is the supershow. There is no higher tier for the promotion to build toward.

The event has no preferred venue configuration, meaning the simulation will use the promotion's default venue (likely Chicago-area venues, once the venues table is populated). The event is not configured for autobooking, meaning the player must book every weekly show manually — a significant booking burden, since 52 shows per year is the highest booking volume of any promotion in the ModzVerse.

Lucha Bellas — Luchastica (Annual)

Luchastica is Lucha Bellas' annual supershow, scheduled for January Week 5 every year. The event runs for 90 minutes with "Huge" importance, and is hosted in North America — preferred country: Mexico (countryID 55). The event name is a portmanteau of "Lucha" and "Fantastic" (or perhaps "Lucha" and "Americana"), positioning it as the premier showcase of lucha libre wrestling in the Western Hemisphere.

Luchastica is not autobooked, meaning the player must book the card manually. The choice of January Week 5 places it in direct competition with RAIBARAZU's Battle of Rivals and Valkyrie Project's AEGIS LEAGUE Finals — three promotions, three supershows, all on the same weekend. A Lucha Bellas player booking Luchastica will need to make the card compelling enough to draw fans away from the competing Tokyo and Saga shows.

RAIBARAZU — Three Annual Supershows + Three Tours

RAIBARAZU hosts six events in total: three annual supershows and three touring cycles. Each supershow is paired with its corresponding tour, and the tour results feed directly into the supershow card. The three supershow–tour pairs are:

Battle of Rivals + Road to Battle of Rivals

Battle of Rivals is RAIBARAZU's flagship event — the equivalent of WrestleMania or Wrestle Kingdom. Scheduled for January Week 5, the event runs for 120 minutes (the longest runtime of any event in the ModzVerse, tied with Fall of Honor) and is configured with "Huge" importance. The event has no preferred venue configuration, suggesting it rotates or is held at RAIBARAZU's home venue.

The Road to Battle of Rivals is the touring cycle that feeds the supershow. It is configured as a Tour with autobook=1 (the simulation will book it automatically) and repeatAnnually=1 (the same tour structure recurs every year). Notably, the Road to Battle of Rivals is the only RAIBARAZU tour without pre-scheduled dates in the eventDates table — suggesting either that the tour is dynamically scheduled by the simulation, or that the dates are a BETA-era gap in the database.

Autumn Blood Moon + Road to Autumn Blood Moon

Autumn Blood Moon is RAIBARAZU's September supershow, scheduled for September Week 5. The event runs for 90 minutes with "Huge" importance, preferred venue: Asia / Japan (countryID 137). The name "Autumn Blood Moon" evokes both the lunar imagery of autumn (the harvest moon tradition) and the crimson of spilled blood — a fitting name for a Pure Wrestling supershow where the in-ring work is hard-hitting and physical.

The Road to Autumn Blood Moon tour is the most fully-scheduled tour in the ModzVerse database. It runs from September 2 to September 27 (12 tour stops, every 2–3 days), all preferred for Asia / Japan. The tour is configured with autobook=1 and repeatAnnually=1, making it a fully automated recurring tour that the simulation will book without player intervention.

Fall of Honor + Road to Fall of Honor

Fall of Honor is RAIBARAZU's October supershow, scheduled for October Week 5. The event runs for 120 minutes (tied with Battle of Rivals for the longest runtime in the ModzVerse) with "Huge" importance, preferred venue: Asia / Japan. The name suggests a thematic continuation from Autumn Blood Moon — the "fall" referring both to the autumn season and to the loss of honor that a worker suffers when defeated. This is likely RAIBARAZU's most storyline-heavy supershow, where grudge matches and "honor vs. honor" feuds pay off.

The Road to Fall of Honor tour runs from October 1 to October 31, 2025 (13 tour stops, every 2–3 days), all preferred for Asia / Japan. Notably, this tour has autobook=0, meaning the player must book it manually — unlike the Road to Autumn Blood Moon, which is autobooked. This is the only RAIBARAZU tour that requires manual booking, and it is the most tour-heavy month on the calendar (13 stops in 31 days, nearly every other day).

Frozen Combat + Road to Frozen Combat

Frozen Combat is RAIBARAZU's February supershow, scheduled for February Week 5. The event runs for 90 minutes with "Huge" importance, preferred venue: Asia / Japan. The name "Frozen Combat" evokes the cold of February (in Japan, February is the coldest month of the year) and the metaphorical coldness of the in-ring work — Pure Wrestling is, by design, a sport-like style where emotion takes a back seat to athleticism.

The Road to Frozen Combat tour runs from February 3 to February 28, 2025 (12 tour stops, every 2–3 days), all preferred for Asia / Japan. Like the Road to Fall of Honor, this tour has autobook=0, meaning the player must book it manually. The February tour is the first tour of the canon year, and the player booking it will set the tone for the entire RAIBARAZU calendar.

Valkyrie Project — Three Autobooked Specials

The Valkyrie Project hosts three annual events, all configured with autobook=1 (the simulation will book them automatically). All three events are preferred for Asia / Japan. The three events form a coherent annual narrative around the AEGIS LEAGUE championship:

Valhalla (January Week 1)

Valhalla is the Valkyrie Project's opening event of the year, scheduled for January Week 1. The event runs for 90 minutes with "Huge" importance. The name — Valhalla, the hall of slain warriors in Norse mythology — positions the event as the gateway to the year's championship narrative. The first week of January is the earliest possible slot for a signature event, making Valhalla the very first event of the ModzVerse canon calendar. This is significant: whoever is booked in the Valhalla main event will be the first worker the world sees in 2025.

Twilight of The GODS (July Week 5)

Twilight of The GODS is the Valkyrie Project's mid-year supershow, scheduled for July Week 5. The event runs for 90 minutes with "Huge" importance. The name — Twilight of the Gods, or Ragnarök in Norse mythology — positions the event as the climactic mid-year moment, the apocalyptic showdown where the championship narrative reaches its peak. This is likely the event where the AEGIS LEAGUE championship is most fiercely contested.

The AEGIS LEAGUE Finals (January Week 5)

The AEGIS LEAGUE Finals is the Valkyrie Project's closing event of the year, scheduled for January Week 5 (the same weekend as Battle of Rivals and Luchastica). The event runs for only 30 minutes — the shortest runtime of any event in the ModzVerse — suggesting it is a tightly-focused championship card, perhaps a single tournament final or a single title match rather than a full supershow. The AEGIS LEAGUE championship is the most likely headline match.

The choice of January Week 5 for the AEGIS LEAGUE Finals is strategic. It places the Valkyrie Project's championship finale in direct competition with RAIBARAZU's Battle of Rivals and Lucha Bellas' Luchastica — three promotions, three supershows, all on the same weekend. The Valkyrie Project's bet is that the championship stakes will draw viewers even against the larger promotions' supershows.
